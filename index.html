Microphone Tip

            

              For best voice recognition, use Google Chrome on desktop or Android and set microphone permission to Allow.
              On a phone the app must be opened over HTTPS (not a plain http:// LAN address) — browsers block voice input on insecure pages.
              The app shell works offline after the first load; translation and speech recognition need an internet connection.
